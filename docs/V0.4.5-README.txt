PHANTOM SEEKER V0.4.5 // PHANTOM GRID AXIS LABEL FIX

WHAT WAS WRONG
==============
The previous V0.4.4 compass patch used a DOM overlay projected from a camera captured outside app.js.
That layer was not reliably attaching to the actual Three.js axes in the public Grid.

V0.4.5 removes that mechanism completely.

NEW METHOD
==========
NORTH / SOUTH / EAST / WEST / UP / DOWN are now REAL THREE.JS SPRITES.

They are inserted directly into the same Three.js scene as:
- the HEN permit sphere
- Guardian markers
- Brain Tree markers
- the X/Y/Z axis lines

The labels sit just beyond the real axis endpoints:
- NORTH  +Z
- SOUTH  -Z
- EAST   +X
- WEST   -X
- UP     +Y
- DOWN   -Y

Because they are Three.js sprites:
- they remain physically attached to the axes while rotating the map
- they always face the camera
- they move and zoom with the map
- they are not screen-corner overlays
- they remain readable above the survey geometry
