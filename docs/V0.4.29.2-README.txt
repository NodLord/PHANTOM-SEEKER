PHANTOM SEEKER V0.4.29.2 // CINEMATIC CLUSTER COMPOSER
=======================================================

BASE
----
Built directly from V0.4.29.1.

GOAL
----
Make the finite-volume architecture feel rich, layered and cinematic again
without returning to a screen-filling purple fog.

SCENE COMPOSER
--------------
- Scene Composer remains deterministic with seed 428291.
- Each region now creates multiple NEBULAR COMPLEXES instead of 2-3 isolated
  primitives.
- Typical logical structure count per region is roughly 18-30.
- Hero logical structure count is ~17.
- Structures are distributed across left/right, near/mid/far and several
  vertical positions.
- Blue and blue-grey dominate; violet is secondary; dark fragments are accents;
  cold rose remains rare.

PERFORMANCE / ACTIVE CULLING
----------------------------
- Shader active capacity: 8 -> 12 finite structures.
- The JavaScript world may contain dozens of logical volumes.
- A diversity-aware culler selects a balanced set of near/mid/far and
  left/right structures for the current camera slice.
- This avoids sending every logical volume to every raymarch sample.

MATERIAL RICHNESS
-----------------
WALL
- multiple correlated lobes;
- internal erosion/cavities;
- mist halo near wall edges;
- several ridge/filament structures.

MIST
- multiple diffuse internal sheets;
- holes remain between sheets;
- faint secondary mist layer.

FILAMENT
- three warped strands per logical filament volume.

SHEET
- two torn membranes at different internal depths;
- fractured tissue;
- secondary sheet mist and boundary filaments.

HERO COMPOSITION
----------------
The Hero remains readable but no longer feels empty:
- layered blue wall complex on the left;
- far blue-grey mist complex upper-left;
- multiple distant violet/blue filaments on the right;
- sparse dark fragments lower-right;
- additional very far blue mist.
The central/right starfield remains the dominant negative space.

COLOUR / LIGHT
--------------
- Each structure keeps a dominant palette.
- Wall, mist and filament material classes now diverge in tone:
  deep/midnight blue, steel/blue-grey, cyan-blue edge light, violet accents.
- Broad local variation adds black/grey structure without averaging the whole
  scene into purple.

- starfield, galaxies, galaxy rotation.
- Home film grain.
