FIX
---
Jump-capable ships no longer run at full speed until the final frame and then stop abruptly.

For FSD trajectories only:
- Cruise speed remains identical to V0.4.27 for most of the route.
- The final approach now uses an integrated smooth velocity falloff.
- Braking begins roughly 20-27% of the remaining flight time before arrival.
- Velocity reaches exactly zero at the FSD charge point.
- The final Bezier control point is stabilised so the ship settles into a clean approach vector rather than making a last-second turn.
- The old forward drift during FSD charge has been removed.
- FSD charging starts only after the hull is fully stationary.
- Engine glow fades with the braking velocity, then intensifies again during FSD charge.

The existing FSD flash, 4-second wake, ship silhouettes, trail persistence and all non-jump trajectories are preserved.
