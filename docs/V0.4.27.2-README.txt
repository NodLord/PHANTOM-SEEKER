PHANTOM SEEKER V0.4.27.2 // WORLD-SPACE FSD HOTFIX
===================================================

BASE
----
Incremental hotfix built from V0.4.27.1. No celestial generation, title/glitch,
Route Map, Phantom Grid or narrative content is modified.

FIXED
-----
- Ships are now anchored to the same Deep Survey Field cameraY/depth model as
  the celestial scene. Scrolling moves them with the world instead of pinning
  them to the viewport.
- Historical engine-trail samples are stored in route space and re-projected
  every frame, so scrolling no longer detaches trails from their ship.
- FSD charge position is re-projected every frame while the ship is stopped.
- FSD wakes store the world anchor/depth of the ship and remain attached to the
  3D background throughout their full lifetime.

FSD VISUAL PASS
---------------
- Stronger initial jump flash.
- Large soft bloom envelope plus a short white-hot core.
- Wake rebuilt as three diffuse layers: outer ionised haze, mid veil and faint
  central filament.
- Feathered/blurred edges replace the old hard filled cone.
- Deterministic charged dust remains inside the wake and fades with it.
- Wake lifetime remains approximately 4.2 seconds.
