DEEP FIELD CHANGES
------------------
- Removes the former .scanlines and .noise visual layers completely.
- Adds a fine animated film-grain lens instead.
- Deep field keeps the darker blue-grey charcoal palette.
- Major celestial objects are heavily biased toward the left/right edges to protect text.
- Galaxies now use pre-rendered logarithmic spiral arm star fields, central bulges and dust lanes.
- Nebulae now use ten procedural morphologies with cold blue, blue-violet and restrained green variants,
  filamentary structure and embedded star-grain detail.
- Top hero scene: middle former left galaxy removed, lower galaxy moved farther down/left,
  upper-right object becomes a pine-green nebula.
- Closing scene: left galaxy moved farther left; right galaxy replaced by a larger blue/violet nebula.
- Solar systems no longer render orbit circles. They show one scintillating star plus 3-6 grey planets
  moving quickly on invisible elliptical orbits.
- Ship traffic is now smaller, faster, more detailed and fully 3D-feeling: horizontal, diagonal and
  receding-horizon trajectories are supported. Each ship leaves a fading engine trail.
- Three lightweight silhouette families remain: Anaconda-like, Krait-like and Caspian-like.
- Scroll parallax and autonomous slow drift remain active.

PERFORMANCE
-----------
- Galaxy and nebula detail is pre-rendered once into reusable offscreen sprites.
- Canvas DPR is capped at 1.45.
- Only a small number of moving ships may be active simultaneously.
- Film grain updates at ~11 fps using a small repeating tile instead of repainting high-resolution noise.
