WHAT CHANGED
------------
GALAXIES
- All galaxy renderers now use the detailed spiral model.
- The hand-placed hero/footer galaxies are no longer screen-fixed. They exist in
  world space and move with the deep camera / scrolling like the procedural field.
- No solid central disk: the stellar envelope fades continuously and the spiral arms
  are built from thousands of individual stars, arm knots and dust lanes.
- Spiral morphologies vary (2/3/4 arms, barred variants, different twist and direction).
- Galaxies are deliberately rarer than nebulae.

NEBULAE
- More nebulae than galaxies throughout the deep field.
- Compact distant clouds rather than full-screen nebula textures.
- Multiple cold survey palettes: cyan-blue, midnight blue, pine green, violet,
  blue-grey and dark/black dust clouds.
- Nebulae are biased toward the sides to protect text readability.
- Hero/footer special nebulae are also world-space and scroll with camera depth.

STARS / CLUSTERS / SYSTEMS
- Denser background star population.
- Clusters remain procedurally varied.
- Survey solar systems are brighter and easier to read.
- Orbit circles remain hidden.
- Central star scintillates; grey planet points orbit visibly at different speeds.

SHIPS
- Existing Anaconda/Krait/Caspian-inspired traffic preserved.
- Longer trails that persist after the hull has moved on.
- Occasional slower passes.
- Occasional closer passes with larger apparent scale.
- Normal traffic remains small and fast, with diagonal / receding trajectories.

FILM GRAIN
- Fine black/white animated grain strengthened substantially.
- Faster grain refresh.
- No scanlines and no analogue-noise layer.

PERFORMANCE
-----------
Still one 2D canvas for the deep field plus one small repeating grain canvas.
No external textures or Three.js were added to the Home background.
