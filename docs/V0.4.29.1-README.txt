PHANTOM SEEKER V0.4.29.1 // ORGANIC VOLUME HOTFIX
==================================================

BASE
----
Built directly from V0.4.29 Scene Composer.

WHY V0.4.29 SHOWED CIRCLES / OVALS
----------------------------------
The Scene Composer itself was correct, but the shader exposed its finite
containers directly:
- WALL and MIST were based on length(q), creating ellipsoids;
- SHEET used length(q.xy), producing an elliptical envelope.
Noise only textured the inside of those primitives, so their geometry was
still clearly visible.

V0.4.29.1 FIX
-------------
The finite volumes are now INVISIBLE CONTAINERS only. They decide where
material is allowed to exist, but their primitive silhouette is never drawn.

WALL
- independent warped X/Y/Z support edges;
- low-frequency topology decides the visible mass;
- internal erosion creates true holes;
- edge ridges become secondary filaments.

SHEET
- the old elliptical length(q.xy) envelope is deleted;
- independent warped X/Y support edges;
- a low-frequency tissue field decides where the membrane survives;
- large tear fields remove sections of the sheet;
- the remaining plane is itself heavily warped.

MIST
- no sphere/ellipse;
- patchy finite support plus broad pockets and internal cutouts.

FILAMENT
- remains a thin tube-like structure, but now uses stronger domain warping,
  length variation and a secondary strand.

HERO COMPOSITION
----------------
Seed remains 428291, but the first region is retuned for immediate validation:
- mid-depth deep-blue wall enters from lower-left;
- small far blue-grey island high-left;
- two distant thin structures on the right;
- no near-volume in the centre;
- Hero target coverage tightened from ~34% to ~30%.

The goal is that no ellipse, circle or clean geometric boundary is perceptible.
If a primitive shape is still recognizable after this patch, the hotfix has
failed and the material support needs another pass.

PERFORMANCE
-----------
Performance Core remains intact.
The existing container bounding boxes still provide cheap early-outs before
organic FBM material is evaluated.
No new canvas, blur pass or full-resolution raymarch is introduced.

WORLD-SPACE TRAFFIC
-------------------
CPU nebula sampling has been updated to the same organic container logic, so
ship/trail/FSD occlusion remains aligned with the visible gas.
