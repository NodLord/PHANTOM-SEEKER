PHANTOM SEEKER V0.4.30.4 // NATURAL CAVITY VOLUME PASS
=======================================================

BASE
----
Built directly from V0.4.30.3.

PRIMARY GOAL
------------
Move closer to the natural 3D feeling of the original V0.4.30 while keeping
the visible corridor introduced by V0.4.30.2.

1. SCROLL DEFORMATION FIX
-------------------------
V0.4.30.3 sampled nebula material at:
    p.y + cameraY * 0.00074

while occupancy remained in pure world-space.

That caused the fine texture to slide against the atlas when scrolling,
producing the impression that walls were deforming / boiling.

V0.4.30.4:
- material noise is sampled directly from p;
- CPU occlusion uses the same fixed world coordinates;
- camera motion changes viewpoint only.

2. ARTIFICIAL WALL HOLE FIX
---------------------------
Two mechanisms could create an unnatural disappearing wall:
- row-by-row coverage quotas / thresholds;
- aggressive internalCarve multiplication.

Changes:
- row-wise quantile coverage masks removed completely;
- side walls now come from continuous organic envelopes;
- internalCarve is much rarer and only reduces wall density by 34%;
- global holes are controlled by the atlas, not material-level cutouts.

3. BACKGROUND CLOUDS TOUCH THE WALLS
------------------------------------
Central layers now use a broad bridge mask extending into the side walls.
There should no longer be a visible empty seam between deep clouds and the
corridor borders.

4. MUCH MORE DEPTH
------------------
Added independent depth planes:

VERY FAR
- w ~ 0.835
- w ~ 0.735
- w ~ 0.635

MID
- w ~ 0.525
- w ~ 0.435

DEEP FILAMENTS
- w ~ 0.790 / 0.665
- w ~ 0.560 / 0.385

NEAR
- w ~ 0.295
- w ~ 0.215

VERY NEAR / FOREGROUND
- w ~ 0.145
- w ~ 0.095

Near and foreground structures remain sparse, asymmetric and heavily
suppressed around the Hero so they never become a permanent veil.

5. MORE NATURAL SIDE WALLS
--------------------------
- no exact left/right coverage quota;
- width is determined by a slowly moving corridor boundary;
- left/right wall amplitudes are independent;
- wall erosion is soft and cannot delete an entire side;
- width changes continuously over the page.

6. COLOUR DEPTH CUES
--------------------
Palette remains entirely in one cold family, but now separates depth more:
- midnight / navy
- deep blue
- cobalt
- cyan blue
- blue-grey
- steel blue
- violet only as a ~3% shadow hint

Far layers bias cooler / greyer.
Near layers bias deeper / darker.
Filaments bias slightly cyan.

7. PERFORMANCE
--------------
No extra GPU raymarch steps were added.
All additional depth complexity is baked once into the existing 3D occupancy
and biome atlases at startup.
