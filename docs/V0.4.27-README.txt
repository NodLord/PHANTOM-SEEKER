WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Constant-speed ship trajectories
   The old easing curve deliberately slowed ships at the end of a path. V0.4.27 replaces it
   with cubic Bezier trajectories sampled into an arc-length lookup table, so apparent speed
   remains nearly constant over the whole flight.

2. Natural exits vs FSD endings
   Non-jump trajectories always terminate beyond the viewport. A separate subset of traffic
   deliberately ends inside an outer survey lane and performs an FSD jump instead of simply
   vanishing.

3. FSD jump effect
   - short charge phase with increasing engine light
   - no frozen hull: the ship drifts slightly forward while charging
   - intense local flash at departure
   - 4-second tapered luminous wake pointing backward from the jump vector
   - wake size automatically follows apparent ship depth/scale

4. Five distinct silhouettes
   ANACONDA / KRAIT / BELUGA / CASPIAN / SIDEWINDER
   Each has its own proportions, hull outline, panel detail and engine positions. Close passes
   reveal more panel lines; distant ships remain readable mainly from silhouette.

5. Engine trails
   Trails are now model-specific and emitted from the actual engine positions rather than the
   hull centre. They persist roughly 2.4-4.0 seconds depending on the pass and decay gradually.

6. Traffic variety
   Cross-screen, diagonal, climbing, descending and receding trajectories remain mixed. Close
   and slow passes are still occasional rather than constant. FSD-capable endings are around
   one third of spawned traffic.

PERFORMANCE
-----------
No new texture assets and no WebGL layer were added. The FSD wake and ship models are Canvas2D
geometry. Trajectory arc lengths are precomputed once at spawn.
